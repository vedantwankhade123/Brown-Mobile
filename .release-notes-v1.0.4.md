# Brown AI Mobile v1.0.4

### What's New

The phone app now matches the desktop assistant much more closely. The keyboard no longer hides what you are typing, answers from small offline models render cleanly, onboarding is short and quiet, and Settings, Help, account controls, voice and PC sync were rebuilt on one dark layout.

#### Answers and input

- **Keyboard-safe inputs.** The composer, attachment row and bottom panels stay above the on-screen keyboard on devices where the system navigation bar draws over the app — previously every bottom field hid under the keyboard.
- **Cleaner markdown.** A heading glued onto the previous sentence by a small model no longer renders as raw `###`, and snake_case identifiers are no longer italicised away.
- **Stable tables and code** in answers, with late streaming tokens suppressed once you press Stop.

#### New screens and controls

- **Help & Support** with in-app guides, plus Privacy Policy and Terms screens inside the app.
- **Rebuilt Settings** and a separate **Preferences** screen, using the same dark cards, headers and system-bar colors as the desktop app.
- **Shorter onboarding** — one native splash, no narration, straight into a fresh chat.
- **Preferences you can see.** Ask Brown to remember something and it becomes a saved preference you can view, edit or forget from the Preferences screen.
- **Backup export and restore** for conversations, saved preferences and memory.
- **Real account deletion.** The chat database, app-downloaded models, caches and preferences are purged through a native module; if any step fails the deletion reports it instead of half-completing.

#### Models, voice and sync

- **Cancellable model downloads** with progress that survives a restart.
- **Kokoro voices** install per voice, so you can download, switch, cancel or delete one voice without pulling the whole set.
- **Gemini answers** are optional: add your own API key in Settings and the available models are discovered from that key.
- **Desktop Sync** pairing and chat transfer reworked to match the desktop app.

#### Diagnostics

Crash and unhandled-promise details — device specs, app version, timestamp and stack trace only, never your prompts, chats or credentials — are cached on the device and reviewed in Help & Support. Reports are rate limited to one per device per minute.

## Known limits

- The binary is production-signed but not verified by an installer-based clean-install run on a fresh device, and answer quality depends on the model you install — the smallest local models still mishandle long recall tests and occasionally refuse harmless creative requests.
- Text, code, CSV and JSON files can be attached to a chat. PDF, Office, image and audio file processing is not available yet.
- Battery and thermal endurance were measured as a single memory snapshot only, and incoming-call interruption was not tested. Voice-transcription accuracy on denial and on-device speech service availability vary by handset.
